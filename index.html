Broker profiles

Important multi-terminal detail

One running MT5 terminal session is logged into one account at a time. To monitor Exness and Deriv simultaneously, install both terminals and configure separate terminal paths in the backend. If both paths are not configured, connect and inspect one broker at a time. Symbol names such as EURUSD, EURUSD.a, XAUUSD or GOLD must be confirmed in each broker's Market Watch.
